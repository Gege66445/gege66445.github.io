Data access and use

This personal tool uses Rclone to access Google Drive file and folder metadata and upload selected local files. It checks destination filenames to skip existing files.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in my local Rclone configuration. Uploaded files are stored in my Google Drive until I delete them. Local logs may contain filenames and transfer details and remain on my computer until deleted.

I do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or AI model training. File transfers occur between my computer and Google Drive. This website does not receive my Drive files or OAuth credentials.

Revocation and deletion

I can revoke access through my Google Account connections, remove the local Rclone configuration and logs, and delete uploaded files through Google Drive. Revoking access does not automatically delete uploaded files.
